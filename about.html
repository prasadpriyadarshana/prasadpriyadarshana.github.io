A little about who I am, the tools I reach for, and the credentials I've earned along the way.

I'm Prasad Priyadarshana, a certified Azure and AWS Data Engineer focused on end-to-end data engineering — pipeline design, data modeling, and business intelligence.

My work centres on building scalable lakehouse architectures and real-time reporting systems that let organisations make decisions from data they can actually trust. Lately I've been going deeper into applied AI, building production RAG systems and agentic workflows on Databricks.

Outside of delivery work I spend time earning certifications, experimenting with new tools in the Azure and AWS ecosystems, and writing about what I learn.
