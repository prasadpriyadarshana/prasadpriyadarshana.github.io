Project Overview

Designed and implemented an ad-hoc Azure-based data warehousing and reporting solution for Microsoft Business Central data, based on an architecture I proposed to meet the client's requirements for cost efficiency and an Azure-centric technology ecosystem.

Architecture

Data Extraction via OData

  • Developed and configured OData APIs within Microsoft Business Central to securely expose required business entities and enable controlled data extraction
  • OData was selected to maintain an Azure-native integration without introducing external connectors

Data Warehousing

  • Azure Synapse Analytics as the core warehousing platform, including a Lake Database linked to the ADLS Gen2 storage layer
  • Synapse Notebooks performing end-to-end data transformation and preparation processes
  • Star-schema analytical data structures designed to support efficient Power BI reporting

Team Leadership

  • Led and supervised the reporting implementation performed by the data/BI team
  • Provided technical direction and ensured alignment with the overall architecture
